### Changed

- `flui_interaction`'s modules are layered (ADR-0217) and each public item has one module path. `routing` splits into `pointer` (hit testing, the interaction lane, routing, capture, mouse tracking, `PointerEventResampler`, `SamplingClock`, wheel preferences) and `focus` (the focus tree, `FocusManager`, traversal). `arena`, `recognizers`, `settings`, `details`, `traits`, `observability`, `velocity` and the velocity half of `processing` are now `gesture`: import `flui_interaction::gesture::{GestureArena, TapGestureRecognizer, Velocity, VelocityTracker, GestureSettings, ...}`. `clipboard::ClipboardHandle` is `text_input::ClipboardHandle`. `DeviceId` and `FocusNodeId` are named from `ids` only. The crate-root re-exports are unchanged.
- Recognizer items that only a per-file module exposed (`TapButton`, `DragStartBehavior`, `ScaleStartDetails`, `ScaleStartMode`, `PanZoomDisposition`, the force-press callbacks and thresholds, `MultiTapDetails`, ...) are exported from `gesture` with the rest.
- Tracing targets follow the module paths: filter recognizers with `flui_interaction::gesture::recognizers` and the arena with `flui_interaction::gesture::arena`.
