### Fixed
- Terminal presentation cleanup now withdraws input registrations, focus, IME, GlobalKey claims, agent access, and rebuild admission even after an earlier callback or destructor failure. After such a failure, or during an unwind, the closing presentation's remaining trees and callbacks are retained and never dropped, and optional disposal is skipped, so the first failure propagates (ADR-0123, ADR-0127). The platform window and accessibility bridge are released in either case; a healthy close drops everything normally.
- Signals owned by a closed presentation refuse creation, reads, and writes with `SignalError::OwnerClosed`; its writer context reports `EventContextError::Detached`. The value or closure a closed graph refuses is dropped normally.
