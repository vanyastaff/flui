### Removed

- `flui-rendering` `RenderTree`: `get_two_mut`, `get_parent_and_children_mut`, `is_ancestor`, `is_descendant`, `path_to_root`, `collect_nodes_needing_layout`, `collect_nodes_needing_paint`, `nodes_mut`, `visit_depth_first_mut`.
- `flui-rendering` `RenderNode`: `box_render_object`, `is_sliver`, `mark_semantics_flag`.
- `flui-rendering` `RenderState`, `RenderFlags` and `AtomicRenderFlags`: `parent_data_as`, `parent_data_as_mut`, `clear_all_flags`, `mark_has_overflow`, `clear_has_overflow`, `has_overflow` and the `HAS_OVERFLOW` flag.
- `flui-rendering` constraints: `BoxConstraints::{clamp_to, is_loose, min_area, max_area}`, `SliverConstraints::{has_remaining_cache_extent, has_remaining_paint_extent, is_scrolled_out_of_view}`, `SliverGeometry::{cache_beyond_layout, non_painted_layout_extent}`.
- `flui-rendering` contexts: `LayoutContext::{layout_all_children, layout_and_position_child, tighten_width, tighten_height, has_unbounded_width, has_unbounded_height}`, the sliver `HitTestContext::{is_within_cross_axis_range, position_minus_main_axis}`, `PaintContext::paint_child_if_present`, `FragmentRecorder::with_paint_anchor`, `ChildState::with_parent_data`, and the `context::intrinsics_test_support` module.
- `flui-rendering` `RenderView`: `composite_frame`, `layer_mut`, `semantic_bounds`, `automatic_system_ui_adjustment`, `set_automatic_system_ui_adjustment`, and `CompositeResult`; `ViewConfiguration::to_logical_size`.
- `flui-rendering` pipeline: `PipelineOwner::{drop_render_child, has_mid_layout_marks, reassemble_idle}`, `PipelineCell::downgrade_for_test`, `VisualUpdateNotifier::{fire_semantics_owner_created, fire_semantics_owner_disposed}`.
- `flui-rendering` parent data: `ContainerParentDataMixin` and the `container` field of `FlexParentData`, `StackParentData` and `MultiChildLayoutParentData` (no render object linked siblings through it; children are the parent's ordered list), `ContainerBoxParentData`, `WrapParentData` and `ListBodyParentData` (use `BoxParentData`), `FlowParentData`, `ListWheelParentData`, `SliverLogicalParentData`, `SliverLogicalContainerParentData`, `SliverPhysicalContainerParentData`, `TextParentData`, `TreeSliverNodeParentData`, `MultiChildLayoutParentData::has_id`, and the `parent_data::{prelude, box, sliver}` modules, `PARENT_DATA_TYPE_COUNT`, `CORE_TYPES`, `CONTAINER_TYPES`, `type_count`, `is_container_type` and `type_usage`.
- `flui-rendering` delegates and traits: `FlowPaintingContext::all_children_painted`, `RenderProxySliver`, `CustomClipper`, `RectClipper` and the `experimental-delegates` feature.
- `flui-rendering` `testing`: `TreeNode::with_sliver_multi_box_parent_data`, `RenderTester::expect_layout_error`, and the `test_support` module with `NoopSliver`.
