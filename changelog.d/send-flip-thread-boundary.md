### Changed

- The UI surface is owner-local, and the compiler says so. Before: views, controllers, listeners, render objects and their callbacks carried `Send` (often `Send + Sync`) bounds that the one owner thread never used, so application state went into `Arc<Mutex<_>>`. Now: none of them is `Send`; `Rc`, `Cell` and `RefCell` work in every callback, on native and on wasm32, and handing a `Signal`, `Writer`, `WriterSource`, `EventCx`, `StateCell`, `ScrollController`, `TextEditingController`, `TabController` or `CupertinoTabController` to `std::thread::spawn` is a compile error naming the type. The handles meant to cross threads stay `Send`: `SignalSender`, `RebuildHandle`, `FrameWaker`, `AppHandle`, `IoFuture`, `ComputeJob`, the host IO and compute pools and the development hooks.
- Listener, animation-status and post-frame callbacks write signals through a `WriterSource` captured in `init_state`, `did_change_dependencies` or a render-object context. Before: they were to receive an `EventCx` once their `Send` bound went. Now: they get no `cx` (`Listenable` lives below the signal graph), and a listener's panic reaches the UI runtime's frame-failure report after the notification round (ADR-0086 §5).
