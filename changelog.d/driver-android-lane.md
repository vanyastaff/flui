### Added

- `flui_driver::lane::android`: the Android lane, which drives a FLUI app on an emulator or a device from any host through `adb`. A contact is held through `input motionevent` (touch, the mouse's primary button), keys are sent at their release as `keyevent` or `keycombination`, printable ASCII is typed with `input text`, a scroll goes through `input mouse scroll`, and a pen goes through a `uinput` device the lane registers where the device lets the shell (`uinput_device`, probed once a session; the pen is refused elsewhere). It refuses a second contact, a sequence that ends with a key down, the keys and chords that leave the app or change the device (home, recents, back, power, volume, the shade, Meta's shortcuts, and the key-map fallbacks Alt or Meta and Escape, Alt, Meta or Control and Space, Control, Alt and Backspace) and a press of any pointer going down at the display's edge, checks that the window it located holds the focus, at the frame and turn it located it at, before each press, key, text and scroll, guards a device by watching `getevent` and runs an emulator unguarded. Positions are order-only. `AndroidLane::start` installs and starts an app with its endpoint and token as intent extras and forwards a loopback port to it; `lane::android::launch` attaches a driver to it; `lane::android::host_device` takes only the device `FLUI_ANDROID_SERIAL` names, and `HostAdb` cannot be built without a serial. `capture_window` reads `screencap`, cut to the app's window.
- `flui_driver::testing::ScriptedAdb`: a device scripted for the Android lane, so its commands, refusals and releases run without a device.

### Changed

- `lane::send_guarded` answers `Error::ReleaseFailed` when the release that ends a run leaves input held, also after the run failed or was interrupted; the run's own failure is its new `first` field, and `effect()`, `may_have_run()`, `is_cannot_verify()` and `missing_capability()` answer from it, so a timed-out action stays `may_have_run` and an interrupted run stays `cannot_verify`. Before, the run's failure hid what stayed held.
- `os_input::admit` refuses OS input on a physical device (`Isolation::Device`) without a user-activity guard, as on the shared desktop.
- Launching an `Artifact::Apk` through `Driver::launch` answers `not_supported` naming `android_launch`: an APK is started through `lane::android::launch`, which names its package and activity.
