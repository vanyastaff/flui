### Added
- `EditableText` shows touch handles: a touch tap shows a handle under the caret as its `CaretHandle` says, a long press selects the word under it (the whole text of an obscured field) with a handle under each end, and dragging a handle moves its end. A key, a mouse or pen press, the focus leaving or an edit the handles didn't make hides them. `EditableText::handles` and `EditableText::caret_handle` set the look and the caret handle per field; `DefaultSelectionHandles::caret_handle` sets the caret handle for a subtree.
- `CaretHandle` says when a field shows a handle under a collapsed caret: `CaretHandle::for_platform` shows one on a tap that hides after 4 s on Android, Linux and Fuchsia, one without a timeout on Windows, and none on iOS and macOS. `HandleSide::Collapsed` names that handle to a `SelectionHandles`.
- `RenderEditable::set_selection_handles` publishes the selection's ends (or the caret) as layer links, the same contract `RenderParagraph` has, and `RenderEditable::caret_rect_at` gives the caret rectangle at a text position.
