### Added

- `flui-layer` holds the retained scene of ADR-0304: a persistent `SceneVersion` (one reference-count increment to clone, sharing every node a patch did not touch), changed by `SceneOp::{Publish, Place, Retire}` patches that `SceneOwner::apply` applies all or nothing, refusing a `SceneNodeId` of another owner or lineage, an unknown or stale one, a node named by two slots, a slot naming its own ancestor and a retired node still in use with a typed `SceneError`. A boundary's content is a `BoundaryContent` of effects, per-render-object `Chunk`s and child slots built by `ContentBuilder`; A `SceneNodeId` carries its owner's `Lineage`, so ids of two scenes never compare equal; `ContentVersion` and `SceneVersionId` never repeat across owners or after `SceneOwner::renew`. Republishing a boundary costs the slots it gains or loses, not the slots it holds. Nothing produces a scene yet; the paint pipeline still builds a `LayerTree` each frame.
